Principles

Three rules for every stone

Zero-knowledge
“We cannot read your data.”

Data is encrypted on your computer with a key derived from your passwords. Only ciphertext ever leaves it.

Open-core
“The core will be open. Privacy is free.”

We will open the core source code for review. Protection is never sold or cut down: the free edition is as secure as the full one.

Offline-first
“Works without the internet.”

No cloud, no analytics, no updates that install themselves. Everything runs on your computer or your office network.
