Chronicle

How Odem was cut

History by version: what went into each one. New entries appear here with every release.

  1. Version 1.2 · current

    Crypto, sync and an open core

    • Crypto wallets: accounts in BTC, ETH, USDT and other coins with the right precision, manual rates, revaluation.
    • A “Staff payouts by channel” report: cash, bank, transfers, crypto wallets.
    • Sync between computers through a shared folder (OneDrive, Yandex Disk, a network share): only ciphertext goes there, one computer writes, a conflict never overwrites data.
    • An open security core: encryption, the signed log and Shamir’s scheme as a separate MIT-licensed package.
    • A single-computer installer that needs no admin rights, and a one-archive transfer kit.
    • Beta: the distribution is sent on request via Telegram @data_diver_ru. Business edition keys are in the Telegram bot @datadiver_shop_bot.
  2. Version 1.1

    A verifiable audit log

    Security

    • The audit log is signed with the installation key (Ed25519). An auditor verifies an exported log without a password.
    • Access log: who opened what, when and from which address.
    • Four-eyes approval for deleting entry documents, templates and recurring operations.
    • Restrictions beyond the role: a user can be denied reports, exports, document viewing, reversals.
    • The recovery key can be split into parts with Shamir’s scheme (for example, any 2 of 3).
    • Before the first run of a new version the program makes a backup by itself.

    Accounting and reports

    • Printing and PDF for reports and the journal.
    • Russian KUDiR ledger (Ministry of Finance order No. 135n) and simplified-tax return in XML (format 5.09).
    • Exports for 1C: “Client-Bank” bank statement and journal entries.
    • Import of counterparties, chart of accounts and opening balances from Excel and CSV.

    Operation and packaging

    • One-click office server: HTTPS and mandatory 2FA set themselves up.
    • English interface, launch from a shortcut.
    • Free edition with full protection: 1 company and up to 3 users.
  3. Version 1.0

    The first stone

    • Double-entry bookkeeping, multiple companies and currencies, user roles.
    • Encryption of all data on disk, 2FA, audit log, backups.
    • Taxes for Russia, Kazakhstan, Belarus, the US and the EU; documents attached to entries.
    • Operation on a local network.
