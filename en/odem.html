Accounting that cannot be read from outside

Check it yourself

Trust you can verify

We don’t ask you to take our word for it. Here is what you can check on your own.

Audit log

Every entry is signed (Ed25519) and chained to the previous one. The distribution includes tools/verify-audit.js: an auditor verifies an exported log without a password and without access to the data. Any forged, deleted or reordered entry shows up.

Automated tests

77 automated tests: encryption and key slots, brute-force protection, 2FA, permissions and four-eyes approval, audit-log signatures and their external verification, Shamir’s scheme, rollback detection, HTTPS with device certificates, security headers.

What it protects against

A stolen disk, copy or backup archive is useless without a password (AES-256-GCM, key derived from passwords with scrypt, held only in memory). Neither the vendor nor any external service gets data or keys. Rolling the data back to an old copy is detected.

What it doesn’t protect against - honestly

An administrator of the computer where the vault is open can read the process memory. Malware on a user’s computer sees what the user sees. Exported files (CSV, XML, PDF) are not encrypted.

Approach

Odem has not a single third-party library: only Node.js and its built-in cryptography. That means no supply-chain attacks through other people’s packages, and everything the program does with data is written in its own code - we will open its core for review.

The app never calls out: no analytics, no cloud, no online updates. The only exception is switched on by the administrator: downloading official exchange rates.
